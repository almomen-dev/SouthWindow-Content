How-to Guide
1
Verify Title Deed

Check the title deed at the local Sub-Registry Office to ensure the seller has clear ownership.

2
Check Khatiyan

Verify the land record (Khatiyan) at the Land Office to confirm the land details.

3
Get RAJUK Approval

Ensure the building has valid RAJUK approval for construction and occupancy.

4
Verify Tax Clearance

Check if all property taxes are paid and up to date.

5
Obtain NOC

Get No Objection Certificate from relevant authorities if required.

6
Review Sale Deed

Carefully review the sale deed and ensure all terms are correct.
