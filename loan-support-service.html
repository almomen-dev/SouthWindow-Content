Frequently Asked Questions
What documents are required for a home loan?

You will need: National ID/Passport, TIN, Salary Certificate/Income Proof, Bank Statements (last 6 months), and Property Title Deed. Additional documents may be required based on bank policy.

What is the maximum loan amount I can get?

The maximum loan amount depends on your income, existing liabilities, and property value. Typically, banks offer up to 80-90% of the property value, subject to your repayment capacity.

What is the loan processing time?

Typically, loan processing takes 5-15 working days from the date of complete document submission. This includes document verification, property valuation, and credit assessment.

What is the interest rate for home loans?

Interest rates vary by bank and typically range from 9% to 12% per annum. The exact rate depends on your profile, loan amount, tenure, and the bank's policy. Our experts will help you find the best rate.

Can I prepay my home loan?

Yes, most banks allow prepayment with minimal charges. Some banks may have a lock-in period of 1-3 years. Check with your bank for specific terms and prepayment fees.
